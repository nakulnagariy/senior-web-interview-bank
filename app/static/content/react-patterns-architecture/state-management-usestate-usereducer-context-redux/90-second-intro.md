# SCOR RUP 90-Second Introduction

Use this in recruiter, hiring manager, or panel intros. Keep delivery at about 150 to 190 words.

## Version 1: Tailored Script

I am a senior frontend engineer with more than six years of experience building and scaling React applications in enterprise environments. A big part of my recent work has been leading Angular to React migrations while keeping feature delivery stable.

My approach is to treat migration as an operating model, not just a rewrite. I start with clear domain boundaries, put a coexistence strategy in place, and move teams through vertical slices with measurable milestones. I have also designed micro-frontend platforms using Module Federation, with strong contracts, failure isolation, and independent deployment pipelines.

From an engineering quality perspective, I focus on performance budgets, strict TypeScript contracts, and practical testing with Jest and React Testing Library. I work closely with platform teams on CI/CD, progressive rollout, and rollback controls, and I am comfortable integrating front-end solutions in Azure ecosystems.

What I enjoy most is aligning technical decisions with business priorities, coaching teams through complex transitions, and delivering modernization with low production risk.

## Delivery Tips

- Speak in outcomes, not only technologies.
- Mention one quantified result if possible.
- Pause briefly after each paragraph-length idea.
- End with why this role is a direct fit.
